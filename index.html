ygo.css

A stylesheet in the materials of Yu-Gi-Oh! cards: card frames, name boxes, text boxes, with hard bevels as in Windows 98. It styles some elements by themselves (body, buttons, fields) and gives classes for the rest. The attribute above switches between the light theme (Normal monster) and the dark theme (Dark Synchro).

<script src="ygo.css/ygo.js"></script>      <!-- in <head>: the theme before the first paint -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="ygo.css/ygo.css">

Card frames

A panel of card frame. The card type is data-card on any element; elements inside take it on (name boxes, buttons, text boxes). Without one: Normal, in the dark theme Dark Synchro.

<div class="card-frame" data-card="trap">...</div>
